The five-second test

who you are, what you offer, who you help

Designers who build coaching sites for a living name the same first mistake: the visitor cannot tell, within five seconds of landing, who the coach is, what they offer and who they help.

Who you are

Your name, your photo and one line of positioning. People hire a person, not a practice name.

What you offer

The shape of working with you, stated simply: one-to-one, group, intensive, program.

Who you help

The person, named. New managers, founders in their first exit, women returning to work, athletes.

Clarity, credibility and one call to action

The ranking guides agree that a coaching website does three jobs: clarity about the problem you solve, credibility through real proof and branding, and a single clear next step. The four mistakes they see most are the opposite of those jobs, and the site is designed to avoid each one.

Everything Your Coaching Website Needs To Convert Successfully Today

Every element is strategically planned to communicate your expertise, strengthen your personal brand, capture qualified leads, and create a clear journey from first visit to booked discovery call.

Your Story & Positioning

AN ABOUT PAGE THAT TELLS YOUR COACHING STORY

The about page is the second most visited page on almost every coaching site. It is written as a story: what you did before, what changed, how you work now and who you are for. Your method or framework is explained simply so the prospect can picture the work, and the page ends with the discovery call, not a contact form.

Content engine

blog, podcast or video, integrated rather than bolted on

If you want a regular stream of visitors, the site needs content people are actually searching for. The site includes a content section built for whichever format you produce: articles organized by niche, podcast episodes with player embeds and show notes, or video. Each piece ends with the same two calls to action, so content grows the list and books calls instead of just being read.

Lead Generation

One lead magnet, connected to the list you already run

One free resource, offered consistently, outperforms five competing opt-ins. The site includes a single lead magnet section, a landing page for it and the email capture, connected to the newsletter or email tool you use. A quiz or assessment can take this role if that suits your practice.

Consulting Expertise

Consulting website design for solo consultants & small firms

Consultants share the coach's problem in a different suit: the buyer needs to understand your expertise, your engagement model and your results before a conversation. Consultant website design under this package covers expertise pages, engagement types, a process section and a clear path to an introductory call. Case study sections are structured so you can add real client work as permissions allow.

Trust & Credibility

Testimonials and proof, real or empty

Social proof is the credibility layer, and it only works if it is real. The site includes a testimonial component structured for client quotes with names and permission, a media and speaking block, and certification logos for credentials you hold. Empty states are designed so the site looks complete before the proof arrives, and nothing is ever filled with placeholder quotes.

Easy Call Booking

A discovery call page, bookable from every page

The site's one primary call to action is a discovery call booked through Calendly. The page explains what the call is for, how long it runs, that it is not a sales pitch, and what happens after. Every page, article and offer ends here.
